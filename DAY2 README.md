DAY2 Tasks: 
- Data Analytics Life Cycle
  Understand Business Problem - Collect Data - Clean Data - Analyze Data - Visualize Data - Communication - Take Action - Monitor.
- Business Problem
  Problem faced by the company in the company goals, customers, operations, and perfomance.
- Data Problem
  What information I used from the data to understand the problem
- Metric
  It is number that measures something 
  Eg: number of visitors= 20000
      revenue = Rs 20 Lakh
- KPIs
   It is imp number that tells us whether the business is reaching the goals
- Descriptive Analysis
   It tells us that what is Happened, look at the past and present data and summarize it
  Eg: Jan sales = 5 lakh
      Feb sales = 3lakh
      Mar sales = 2 lakh
  DA says : sales are decreased by 1 lakh in march 
- Diagonstic Analysis
  It tells us Why it happened gives the reason why sales decreased by 1 lakh in march
-Predictive Analysis
  It predicts what might happens in future using the past and present data
- Prespective Analysis
  It tells us what should we do
- Analytical thinking 
  It means breaking a big problem into smaller questions and using data to find the answer.
- Requirement Clarification
  It means asking questions to clearly understand what the client or manager wants.
- Core Framework
  Business Question	: What problem are we solving?
  Define Metric : What number will we measure?
  Identify Data :	What information do we need?
  Collect Data : Where can we get it?
  Validate : Can we trust the data?
  Clean	: Can we fix the data?
  Analyze	: What is happening in the data?
  Visualize :	How can we show it clearly?
  Insight :	What did we learn?
  Recommendation : What should we do?
  Business Decision :	What will the company actually do?
