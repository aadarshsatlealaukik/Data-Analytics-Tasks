# Alaukik Sales Dashboard & Sales Analysis

## 1. Project Overview

The **Alaukik Sales Dashboard** is an Excel-based sales analytics project designed to analyze sales performance, revenue generation, salesperson performance, regional contribution, product performance, and deal outcomes.

The project transforms sales data into meaningful business information through Excel formulas, PivotTables, charts, and an interactive dashboard.

## 2. Project Objectives

- Analyze overall sales revenue and deal performance.
- Calculate the number of total, won, lost, and pending deals.
- Evaluate the overall sales win rate.
- Identify top-performing products and regions.
- Analyze monthly revenue trends.
- Compare salesperson performance.
- Understand the major reasons behind lost deals.
- Support business decisions using sales data.

## 3. Tools & Technologies

## 4. Dataset Description

The workbook contains the following sheets:

| Sheet | Description |
|---|---|
| Sales | Main sales transaction dataset containing 50 sales records |

| Products | Product names, categories, and prices |
| Salespeople | Salesperson details, teams, regions, and managers |
| Targets | Monthly salesperson sales targets |
| Date | Date-related information for analysis |
| Sales Dashboard | Main sales performance dashboard |
| Win Rate Analysis | Won deals, lost deals, and win rate by salesperson |
| Lost Deals Analysis | Analysis of reasons behind lost deals |
| Pivot Sheets | Supporting PivotTable analysis |
| Chart Sheets | Supporting charts and visualizations |
| Excel_Tasks | Excel practice tasks and completion tracking |

## 7. Key Findings

Based on the dashboard:

- The business recorded total revenue of ₹51,45,784.50.
- Out of 50 deals, 29 were won.
- The overall win rate was 58%.
- The remaining deals consisted of 14 lost deals and 7 pending deals.
- Revenue is analyzed across six months, from January to June 2026.
- The workbook includes salesperson-level win-rate analysis and lost-deal reason analysis.

## 8. Business Recommendations

Based on the available analysis, the business can:

1. Investigate why 14 deals were lost and develop strategies to improve conversions.
2. Review competitor-related losses and strengthen the sales strategy.
3. Evaluate pricing concerns to understand whether pricing adjustments or better value communication are needed.
4. Study high-performing products to identify opportunities for additional sales.
5. Monitor monthly revenue and salesperson performance against targets.
6. Follow up on pending deals to improve the chances of conversion.
